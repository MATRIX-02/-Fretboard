🎵 Reference tuner

These are real plucked-string tones, not beeps — so they sound like your guitar and are much easier to match against. Play the reference, then your string, and turn the peg until the wobble between them disappears.

Tuning tip: tune up to the note, never down to it. If you're above the pitch, drop below first and come back up — that takes the slack out of the machine head so the string holds its tuning.

🎼 Chord library

Click any diagram to hear it. Use "one string at a time" to check your own chord against it — that's how you find a string that isn't ringing.

🪘 Strumming patterns

Hear a pattern played on a real chord, watch which stroke lands where, then play along. D = down, U = up, – = skip (hand still moves), ✕ = muted chuck.

🔄 Progression player

Build a chord loop and play along with it. Useful for practising changes in time, and for hearing what a progression sounds like before you try to play it.
