Streak insurance

On a bad day, do the Short session — Tier 1 only, about 15 minutes. It keeps the streak and the physical adaptation alive. A missed day costs more than a small day.

Why a fixed time

Decision-making is the expensive part. If practice happens "when I have time", you spend willpower every single day deciding. A fixed slot and a guitar left on a stand removes both costs.
