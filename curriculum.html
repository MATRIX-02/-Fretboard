48 weeks, beginner to advanced

Four phases. Each week has a focus, a set of modular practice blocks, a theory idea, a song target and one measurable milestone. Blocks are tiered so a 15-minute day and a 90-minute day both count.

How the daily plan works

Tier 1 — Core

The non-negotiables: warm-up, tuning, and the week's single most important drill. Roughly 15–25 minutes. On a bad day, do only this and the streak still counts.

Tier 2 — Standard

Adds application drills and song work. Total 40–55 minutes. This is the pace the 48-week timeline assumes.

Tier 3 — Deep

Adds creative work, recording, and repertoire. Total 75–95 minutes. Use on weekends or when you're in the mood.

Realistic timeline. At 5 sessions a week you finish in about 11 months. At 7 sessions a week, around 9 months — the extra speed comes from faster physical adaptation, not from skipping content. Weeks 8, 20, 34 and 48 are checkpoints: don't move past one until you've hit its milestone.
